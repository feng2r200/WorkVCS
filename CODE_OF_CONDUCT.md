# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in the WorkVCS community should be respectful, constructive, and
focused on improving the project. Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and helping others succeed;
- protecting private information and honoring project boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through a private channel
listed on the GitHub profile for `feng2r200`. Reports will be reviewed fairly
and confidentially to the extent practical. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
ban participants when behavior threatens a safe and productive community.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Attribution
